Start here

Do this once on each phone, at home on Wi-Fi.

  1. Open hogcreekhc.github.io in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Type the personal code the club gave you and tap Sign in. Your code is yours alone; don't share it.
  3. iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen. Open it from that icon from now on.
  4. When it asks, allow location and motion & orientation. Location puts your blue dot on the map; motion lets the cone point the way you're facing.
  5. Go to Settings (the last tab; swipe the tab row left to reach it). Under Offline map, tap Save the lease map for no-signal use. The lease has about one bar, so do this before your first trip. See No signal.
Old icon? If your home-screen icon opens the old address, delete it and add the app again from hogcreekhc.github.io.

Hunt plan: where to sit

Hunt plan ranks the stands for the day and time you pick, using the wind forecast, the time of day and the rut dates.

  1. Pick a day at the top: today or the next few days.
  2. Read Best stands. The top pick has the right wind and the best record for that time of day. Each card says why.
  3. Slide Scent cones to an hour, or tap Now. The shaded cones on the map show where your scent drifts from each stand. Pick a stand whose cone doesn't cover where deer come from.
Rule of thumb: deer come from upwind. If the cone from your stand covers the trail you're watching, sit somewhere else that day.

Who's out: check in and share

Check in at your stand

  1. When you get to your stand, open Who's out and tap Check in to a stand. If you're close to a stand, the app offers it by name.
  2. Everyone arriving at camp can now see where you are, so nobody walks in on you.
  3. When you leave, tap Check out. The app asks how the sit went (see Log). A check-in clears itself after 8 hours if you forget.

Share your location (optional)

  • Share my location while I'm on the property: your dot shows to the club only while you're on the lease. It stops when you leave.
  • Share everywhere for 8 hours: for a drive in or a drag, then it turns itself off.
  • With both off, only you see your dot.

Reserve a stand

  1. Open Who's out, find Reservations, and tap Reserve a stand.
  2. Pick the day and the stand, then tap Reserve.
  • Reserve from the property (at camp or on the lease), for today or the night before.
  • One stand per hunt.
  • A morning stand is held until 30 minutes after shooting light. Not checked in by then? It's open to anyone.
  • An evening stand is held until 30 minutes after the evening hunt starts.
  • Can't make it? Remove your reservation so someone else can sit there.

Log what you see

The more the club logs, the smarter Hunt plan gets. Everything here goes to the whole club.

Log a sit

  1. Check out of your stand, or tap Log a sit in Log.
  2. Fill in times, does and bucks seen, other game, and a short note. Wind is saved for you.
  3. Saw a deer move? Tap + Add a sighting and draw its arrow (next).

Log a sighting (with an arrow)

  1. Tap Log a sighting, then tap the map where the deer was.
  2. Tap again where it went. That draws the arrow. Tap Next.
  3. Add how many, buck or doe, points, when, and what it was doing. Save sighting.

Arrows show on the map under the Sightings layer. Over a season they show exactly how deer travel the lease.

Add a harvest

  1. Tap Add a harvest. Pick the species and sex, points, weight, date, time and stand.
  2. Add a photo. Notes like "Shot at 5:20, ran 40 yd" help everyone.
  3. Made a mistake? Open the harvest and edit it.
Counting points: count every tip at least 1 inch long, brow tines included.

Trail cam photos

  1. Tap a Trail cam pin, then Add photos, and choose pictures from your phone.
  2. The date and time come from the photo. Tag them Buck, Doe, Hog, Turkey or Other, then Save photos.

Stands by the numbers, Harvests this season, Recent sits and Deer sightings at the bottom of Log show what every stand has produced.

The map

Base map

Aerial (newest · Sept 2025) for timber and openings as they are now, Aerial (sharper, older) for more ground detail (older, so cuts and replanting may be out of date), LiDAR terrain for ridges, ditches and old roads, Topo for creeks and contour lines.

Overlays

Switch layers on and off in Map: Boundary, Stands, Scent cones, Sightings, Deer trails, Roads, Walk-in routes, Tracks, Club pins, Harvests, Work list, Camp, Hunters, Neighbor lines, Public land (WMA), Elevation (high / low), 10 ft contours, Old stand pins.

Neighbor lines are traced from onX and can be off by about 30 m. Know the line before you shoot toward it.

Tools

  • Measure distance / area: tap 2 points for a distance, 3 or more for acres.
  • Zoom to property: brings the whole lease back on screen.
  • Open this spot in Apple Maps (Google Maps on Android): opens the map center in your phone's maps app for driving directions.
  • Draw a deer trail / Draw a road: tap along it in order, then save. The whole club sees them.
  • Tap a stand on the map for its winds, facing, why it's good and how to walk in.

Blood trail

  1. In Map, tap Start a blood trail where the deer was standing when you shot. Your path draws in red.
  2. Every time you find sign, tap Blood.
  3. Lost it? Tap Last sign to walk back to the last blood you marked, then circle out from there.
  4. Found your deer? Tap End. The red line stays on the map until you start a new trail.
Keep the app open while you track. Your phone stops GPS when the screen locks.

Tracks and walk-in routes

Keep your screen on the whole time you record. iPhone stops GPS when the screen locks or you switch apps, and your track will have gaps. Use a battery pack for long walks.
  1. In Map, tap Start a track. A Recording bar shows at the top.
  2. Walk. Tap Stop when you get there.
  3. Name it and choose: Save track (just on your phone), Save as deer trail or Save as road (shared with the club).
  4. To make it the quiet way in for a stand, open the track and tap Set as walk-in route. Everyone then sees it on that stand.

Your saved tracks are listed under Saved tracks in Map. Delete one, or Clear all tracks.

No signal on the lease

Save the map before you go

  1. At home on Wi-Fi, open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (the last tab; swipe the tab row left to reach it).
  3. Under Offline map, tap Save the lease map for no-signal use.
  4. Keep the app open while it counts up the map pieces. It takes a few minutes. When it says Lease map saved, you're done.

It saves the whole lease and a margin around it, zoomed all the way in: both aerials, LiDAR terrain, Topo, the 10 ft contours and the elevation shading. After that the button reads Update saved map. Tap it again if the map looks blurry or the app says there are new maps, and if it says only some pieces saved, run it again on better signal.

On the lease

  • GPS works with no signal. Your dot, the map, stands and tools all work.
  • Pins, sits, check-ins and harvests save on your phone and upload as soon as you get a bar. Don't enter them twice.
  • Wind and weather need a signal. With none, the app shows the last forecast it got.

Work list

  1. In Work, tap Add a job, such as "Cut shooting lanes at S2".
  2. Add details and who's doing it. Pin it on the map if it's somewhere specific.
  3. Mark it done when it's finished. Done jobs stay listed for 60 days.

Settings

  • See who you're signed in as, or Sign out of this phone.
  • Help: open this Field guide or Watch the intro.
  • Rut starts / Rut ends help Hunt plan weigh midday sits.
  • Offline map: Save the lease map for no-signal use (see No signal).

For the admin

  • Properties: draw a lease by tapping corners or walking the line with GPS. New tracts show on everyone's map.
  • Stands: add or edit a stand: type, facing, good winds, ratings for mornings and evenings, kids OK, and how to walk in.
  • Members are added or removed in the club sheet's Members tab. Removing a row locks that phone out on its next sync.

Club rules the app follows

  • Check in when you get to your stand, and check out when you leave.
  • Reserve from the property, for today or the night before. One stand per hunt.
  • Morning reservations open up 30 minutes after shooting light if you haven't checked in.
  • Delete only your own pins. The admin cleans up the rest.
  • Wear a harness in every tree stand, and on kids every time.
  • Know where everyone is checked in before you shoot or walk.

Help

My code doesn't work

Check for typos. If the club changed it, ask the admin for your new one.

My cone points the wrong way

Allow motion & orientation in your phone's settings for your browser. Hold the phone flat, top pointed where you're facing. Wave it in a figure-8 to calibrate. Near a metal stand the compass can drift.

The map is blank on the lease

You need to save the map at home first: Settings → Offline map → Save the lease map for no-signal use. How

My pin or sit isn't showing for others

It's waiting on your phone for a signal. Open the app once you have a bar and give it a minute.

My track has a straight line in it

Your screen locked and GPS paused. Keep the app open and the screen on while recording.

Something else is broken

Tell Jake. A screenshot helps.
